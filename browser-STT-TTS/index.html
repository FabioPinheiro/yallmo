Control from the REPL

  • m: record browser microphone. Space stops and transcribes; Esc discards. Maximum 30 seconds.
  • t: speak the latest assistant text once. A new speech command replaces previous speech.
  • v: existing JVM microphone, unchanged.

Privacy and browser limits

The microphone remains enabled until you Disable audio, leave this page, or lose the connection. Samples are captured only on a REPL recording command, held in memory, and sent to the REPL on stop. Cancelled recordings are discarded. The REPL uses your selected transcription provider; no recording is saved by this page.

Only explicitly requested assistant text is sent for browser speech. The browser/OS speech engine may use network services: local-only speech is not guaranteed. “Speaking” means the browser reported a start, not verified audible output.

Permission, audio activation, background execution and speech support vary by browser/OS. Sleep, background throttling, revoked permission or a stalled speech engine may disconnect audio. Focus this tab and click Enable audio again; failed commands are never retried automatically. Only one setup tab can be connected.

Use the exact loopback URL from the REPL. The setup token is kept only in memory and removed from the address bar; reopen the original URL after reload. Do not share it.
